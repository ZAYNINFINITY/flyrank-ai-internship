## What needs fixing / improving

### 1. Cat model — looks boxy and weird

**Current:** `components/three/museum-cat.tsx` — procedural from BoxGeometry/SphereGeometry. Looks like a Minecraft cat, not a real museum cat.

**What to do:**
- Replace with a more realistic procedural cat OR find a free GLTF cat model to load
- If procedural: use smoother geometry (more subdivisions), better proportions, proper fur color gradient
- The cat should feel alive — sitting on the welcome mat near the entrance, tail curling, occasionally looking around
- Reference: museum cats in real galleries are sleek, elegant, usually tabby or black
- Position: currently at `[0.7, 0, approach.minZ + 2.55]` on the welcome mat

### 2. Curator — currently a chatbox, should be a 3D character

**Current situation:**
- `CuratorFigure` in walkable-world.tsx (line ~2017) is a billboard sprite that faces the camera
- When you walk near it and press E, it opens `ChatPanel` as a full-screen overlay
- The curator chat works (OpenRouter + Gemini + exhibitLookup tool) but the UI is a flat chat interface

**What to do:**
- Make the curator feel like a PERSON you're talking to, not a chatbot widget
- The 3D curator figure (billboard sprite) should have better visuals — currently it's a simple plane with a procedurally generated face texture
- When activated, the chat panel should feel like a conversation with a museum guide, not a support ticket
- Add speech bubble styling or a museum-guide card instead of raw chat messages
- The curator model/texture needs work — make it look like an actual museum curator (elegant, professional), not a random face

**Key files:**
- `components/three/walkable-world.tsx` — `CuratorFigure` component (line ~2017)
- `components/ai/chat-panel.tsx` — the chat UI
- `app/api/chat/route.ts` — OpenRouter endpoint

### 3. Weather systems — need better logic

**Current:** `components/three/entrance-environment.tsx` — particle systems for rain/snow/leaves/blossoms. They work but:
- Rain falls in a small area, doesn't feel atmospheric
- No wind effect on particles
- No ground puddles/snow accumulation
- Leaves don't tumble realistically
- Blossoms are just pink circles

**What to do:**
- Add wind offset to all particle systems (diagonal rain, swaying leaves)
- Rain: add splash particles at ground level, darken the scene slightly
- Snow: add slight accumulation effect (white plane that grows at bottom)
- Leaves: make them tumble/rotate more naturally, vary sizes more
- Blossoms: add slight glow/translucency
- Consider: the weather should affect the entrance area's mood lighting too (rain = darker, snow = brighter)

### 4. Flooring at reception — needs fix

**Current:** Reception floor at `walkable-world.tsx` line ~2588 is a simple colored plane with a center runner. The PBR floor textures load for the approach but reception uses a flat `PALETTE.receptionFloor` color.

**What to do:**

- The center runner (accent-colored strip) is there but the surrounding floor looks bland
- Check that the floor connects seamlessly between approach → reception → corridor

### 5. Add a clock synced with system time
